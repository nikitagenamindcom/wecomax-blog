# Callbot by Wecomax

<img src="images/callbot.png" width="96" align="right" alt="Callbot">

**An AI employee for small businesses.** Callbot answers your clients 24/7 in Zalo, Telegram and WhatsApp, books them for a time,
calls them back, keeps your CRM in order — and works on your own computer like a person would: apps, browser, files.

- 🌏 Built in Vietnam for Southeast Asia: Vietnamese, Thai, Khmer, Russian, English and more
- 💬 One assistant for every messenger your clients use
- 📅 Bookings, reminders after 1 and 3 days, a «lost clients» audit
- 🖥️ Works on your Mac: opens apps, writes, calls, reports back to your phone
- 🔒 Your data is your own room: it never reaches another customer

## The story

Callbot started in Dalat, Vietnam, from a simple problem: small hotels and shops lose clients because nobody answers at night,
on the weekend, or in the client's language. We built an assistant that never sleeps, speaks the client's language and does the
routine work itself — so the owner can run the business instead of the inbox.
