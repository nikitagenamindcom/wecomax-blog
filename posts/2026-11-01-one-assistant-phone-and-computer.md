# One assistant for your phone and your computer

*1 November 2026*

Most AI chats live in a box: you ask, they answer, and the real work is still yours.
Callbot by Wecomax is built the other way around — **it works where your business already lives: your phone and your computer.**

**What it does today**

- **Answers right away.** Simple questions, translations and lookups go to a fast model and come back in seconds, with sources.
- **Works on your Mac.** Ask from your phone, and the assistant on your computer opens apps, reads and writes files, uses the browser and reports back to your phone — with a live line showing what it is doing.
- **Keeps your clients in one place.** Messages from Zalo, Telegram and WhatsApp land in one CRM, translated into your language, with the original underneath.
- **Draws and films.** Pictures in a few seconds, short videos on request.
- **A team, not one bot.** Virtual employees — a sales manager, a marketer, a free-PR specialist — each with their own daily job, running side by side.
- **Your room is yours.** Your files, chats and personal rules never reach another customer, and nobody else's task can touch your computer.

**What it is learning now (beta)**

Writing first messages to new clients in their language and calling them back by itself, through the messenger apps on your computer.
It already prepares the messages and asks you before sending; full hands-free outreach is the next step.

**How it thinks**

Under the hood is our own skill book: about 700 skills and 21,000 trigger words in more than ten languages.
A request finds its skills in a fraction of a millisecond; when no word matches, the system finds them by meaning.
Only the skills a request needs travel with it — so any model, cheap or strong, works the way we taught it.
